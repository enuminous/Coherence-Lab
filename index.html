Interpretation

EFMW did not establish superiority. Explicit safety rules detected 54/54 scripted failures without false alarms in this scenario design. The EFMW candidate detected 12/54, with 4/36 normal episodes raising alerts.

These are synthetic scenarios and cannot establish real-world AI-agent monitoring performance, nor an EFMW-specific human memory mechanism. Thresholds were calibrated to 5% of normal calibration timepoints, not matched held-out episode false-alarm rates. Conventional multiscale predictors have not been exhaustively eliminated.
